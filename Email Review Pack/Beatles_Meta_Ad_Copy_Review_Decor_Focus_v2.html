Room / decor 6

The Beatles — white room and dog

Four familiar faces, one very happy home.

The Beatles on the wall, the dog by your feet. Plenty to talk about.

To become the Fab Four, they had put in the hours. Hundreds of gigs, including 292 appearances at Liverpool’s Cavern Club alone. Even after a show, the work carried on. In a Newcastle hotel room, John and Paul sat on twin beds with their guitars, working on “She Loves You”. They finished it back at Paul’s house in Liverpool, where an unexpected critic was waiting.

Paul’s dad Jim had one small improvement… Could they not replace those American-sounding yeahs with a nice English yes?

Thankfully, they didn’t take his advice. Beatlemania might have sounded rather polite.

From the previously unseen private archive of renowned photographer Willoughby “Gus” Gullachsen. A numbered, authenticated edition of 100, printed on fine-art paper with archival ink.

More than decor, this is a warm focal point for the room: a little music history, a good story and plenty for visitors to talk about.
